Rentry ("we", "us", or "our") helps people find long-term rental and for-sale properties in Tbilisi, Georgia. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through rentry.ge, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

When you submit our contact form or message us directly, we collect the information you choose to share: your name, phone number or WhatsApp contact, budget range, rental or purchase preferences, and any comments about the property you're looking for. We do not require an account or collect payment information — our service is free for tenants and buyers.

How We Use Your Information

We use the details you provide to match you with suitable properties, contact you about your inquiry, and coordinate viewings, negotiations, and the paperwork involved in renting or buying a property in Tbilisi. We may also use your contact details to follow up after you move in, in line with the long-term support described on our site.

How We Share Your Information

We share your inquiry details only with the property owners or landlords relevant to your search, and only as needed to arrange viewings or finalize a deal. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for marketing purposes.

Cookies and Local Storage

Rentry.ge does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. We use your browser's local storage only to remember your preferred language (English, Georgian, or Russian) so the site displays correctly the next time you visit. This information stays on your device and is never sent to our servers.

Data Retention

We keep your inquiry details for as long as needed to help you find a property, and for a reasonable period afterward in case you return to us. You can ask us to delete your information at any time using the contact details below.

Your Rights

You can ask us, at any time, to tell you what information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below and we'll respond as quickly as we can.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, contact us at +995 593 240 306.
